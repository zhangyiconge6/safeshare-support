简体中文

感谢使用一点长图。App 可以把截图或录屏整理成长图，并在分享前帮助你查看可能需要隐藏的内容。所有处理都在你的设备上完成。

开始使用

  1. 在首页点按“选择图片或录屏”。
  2. 通过 iOS 系统照片选择器选择图片或一段录屏。
  3. 等待 App 在设备上处理所选内容。
  4. 检查处理后的图片，并在分享或保存前确认整张图片。

处理图片

选择多张连续截图时，一点长图会尝试把它们整理成长图。你可以在需要时进入“调整长图”，修改保留范围或图片拼接位置。

如果所选图片无法可靠地整理成长图，App 可能会分别处理这些图片。请在“分享前检查”中逐张确认。

处理录屏

选择录屏后，App 会从录屏内容生成长图。生成后会先显示“检查长图”，让你确认画面是否完整，以及是否存在明显重复或错位。

这一步是有意保留的人工确认。你可以继续前往“分享前检查”，也可以调整录屏使用范围或长图拼接位置。

分享前检查

一点长图可能会列出“建议位置”，帮助你查看可能需要隐藏的内容。点按建议位置可以切换“已隐藏”或“未隐藏”，也可以打开建议位置列表逐项查看。

自动检查可能遗漏内容。 “未发现可能需要隐藏的内容”不表示图片中一定没有隐私信息。分享前请检查整张图片。

手动添加或调整隐藏区域

在“分享前检查”中，长按图片可以手动添加隐藏区域。长按现有区域可以调整它的位置和大小。应用更改后，再检查处理后的图片。

保存与分享

点按“保存到相册”时，App 会请求仅向照片图库添加内容的权限。一点长图不需要读取你的整个照片图库。

点按“分享”会打开 iOS 系统分享面板。只有当你选择目标 App 后，处理后的图片才会提供给该 App。原图和原录屏不会被修改。

常见问题

一点长图需要账户或网络连接吗？

不需要账户。核心图片和录屏处理不需要连接开发者服务器，也不会上传你选择的内容。如果所选素材仅储存在 iCloud，iOS 可能需要联网下载；将结果分享给其他 App 后，该 App 也可能根据自身功能使用网络。

为什么 App 没有把图片整理成长图？

所选图片之间可能没有足够连续的共同内容，或内容不适合整理成长图。App 可能会分别处理这些图片，你仍可逐张检查、隐藏、保存或分享。

为什么长图中出现重复或错位？

快速滚动、弹窗、固定悬浮内容或反复出现的画面都可能影响长图结果。请返回“检查长图”确认，并在需要时调整录屏范围或长图。

没有建议位置，是否代表可以直接分享？

不是。自动检查可能遗漏内容。请始终在分享或保存前检查整张图片，并按需要手动添加隐藏区域。

保存失败怎么办？

请在 iPhone“设置”中确认允许一点长图添加照片，然后返回 App 重试。保存失败不会修改原图或原录屏。

处理期间创建的临时文件会怎样？

App 会在处理录屏或准备系统分享时创建临时文件，并在当前任务结束或分享面板关闭后清理。

English

Thank you for using ScrollOne. The app can turn screenshots or a screen recording into a long image, then help you review content that may need hiding before you share. Everything is processed on your device.

Getting Started

  1. Tap Select Photos or Screen Recording on the Home screen.
  2. Choose photos or one screen recording with the iOS system photo picker.
  3. Wait while the app processes your selection on your device.
  4. Review the processed image, then check the full image before sharing or saving.

Working with Photos

When you choose a sequence of screenshots, ScrollOne tries to combine them into a long image. If needed, use Adjust Long Image to change the content to keep or the image join.

If the selected photos cannot be reliably combined, the app may process them separately. Review each image in Review Before Sharing.

Working with a Screen Recording

After you choose a screen recording, the app creates a long image from its content. Review Long Image appears first so you can check whether the result is complete and look for obvious repeats or content that seems out of place.

This confirmation step is intentional. You can continue to Review Before Sharing, adjust the part of the recording used, or adjust the long image.

Review Before Sharing

ScrollOne may show Suggested Areas to help you review content that might need hiding. Tap a suggested area to change its status between Hidden and Not Hidden, or open the list to review suggestions one by one.

Automatic review may miss content. “No content that may need hiding was found” does not mean the image contains no private information. Check the full image before sharing.

Adding or Adjusting a Hidden Area

In Review Before Sharing, touch and hold the image to add a hidden area manually. Touch and hold an existing area to adjust its position and size. After applying changes, review the processed image again.

Saving and Sharing

When you tap Save to Photos, the app requests permission to add photos only. ScrollOne does not need access to your entire photo library.

Tapping Share opens the iOS system share sheet. The processed image is made available to another app only after you choose a destination. Your original photos and screen recordings are not modified.

Common Questions

Does ScrollOne require an account or an internet connection?

No account is required. Core photo and screen recording processing does not connect to developer-operated servers, and the content you select is not uploaded. If a selected item is stored only in iCloud, iOS may need a connection to download it. After you share a result with another app, that app may also use the internet under its own practices.

Why weren’t my photos combined into a long image?

The selected images may not contain enough continuous shared content, or they may not be suitable for combining. The app may process them separately so you can still review, hide, save, or share each image.

Why does the long image contain repeated or misplaced content?

Fast scrolling, pop-ups, fixed overlays, or repeated content can affect the result. Return to Review Long Image to check it, then adjust the screen recording range or long image if needed.

Is it ready to share if there are no suggested areas?

Not necessarily. Automatic review may miss content. Always check the full image before sharing or saving, and add hidden areas manually when needed.

What should I do if saving fails?

In iPhone Settings, make sure ScrollOne is allowed to add photos, then return to the app and try again. A failed save does not modify your original photos or screen recording.

What happens to temporary files created during processing?

The app creates temporary files while processing a screen recording or preparing the system share sheet. It clears them when the current task ends or the share sheet closes.
