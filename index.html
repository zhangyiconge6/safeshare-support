把连续内容整理成一张长图

  • 将多张连续截图或图片自动拼接成长图。
  • 从一段屏幕录制生成长图。
  • 生成后检查结果，并按需要调整保留范围和拼接位置。

Turn continuous content into one long image. Combine screenshots or photos, create a long image from a screen recording, then review the result and adjust the kept range or image joins when needed.

分享前检查，由你决定

一点长图可以标出可能需要隐藏的建议位置。你可以逐项选择隐藏或保留，也可以手动添加和调整隐藏区域。自动检查只是辅助建议；分享或保存前，请自行确认整张图片。

Review before sharing, with you in control. ScrollOne can suggest areas that may need hiding. You decide what to hide or keep, and you can add or adjust areas manually. Automatic review is an aid and may miss content, so check the full image before sharing or saving.

在设备上完成核心处理

无广告，无需账户。长图生成和隐私检查在设备本地完成，不会把你选择的内容上传到开发者服务器，也不会修改原始图片或录屏。

No ads and no account required. Long-image creation and privacy review happen on your device. ScrollOne does not upload the content you select to developer-operated servers or modify your originals.
