简体中文

生效日期：2026 年 7 月 27 日

一点长图（ScrollOne）尊重你的隐私。本隐私政策说明你使用 App 时，所选图片、录屏和相关信息如何被处理。

处理范围

App 仅处理你通过 iOS 系统照片选择器主动选择的图片或录屏。App 不会浏览或读取你的整个照片图库。

如果你选择将处理后的图片保存到照片图库，App 会请求“仅添加照片”权限。此权限只用于保存你明确选择保存的结果。

设备端处理

长图生成、内容检查和隐藏区域处理均在你的设备上完成。一点长图不会将你选择的图片或录屏上传到开发者运营的服务器，也不使用远程分析、广告 SDK 或跟踪服务。

原始内容与临时文件

一点长图不会修改你的原始图片或原始录屏。处理录屏和准备分享内容时，App 可能会创建临时文件。这些临时文件会在当前处理任务结束或系统分享面板关闭后清理。

只有当你明确选择保存时，处理后的图片才会添加到你的照片图库，并由你继续管理。

系统分享面板与第三方 App

只有在你主动打开 iOS 系统分享面板并选择目标 App 后，处理后的图片才会提供给该 App。目标 App 如何处理这些内容，受其自身隐私政策和服务条款约束；这部分处理不由一点长图控制。

自动检查的限制

分享前请自行确认。 自动检查可能遗漏需要隐藏的内容，也可能把不需要隐藏的位置列为建议。建议位置不代表已经确认的隐私问题。

请在分享或保存前检查所有建议位置、隐藏区域以及整张图片。你可以手动添加或调整隐藏区域。

数据收集与跟踪

当前版本不收集你的个人数据、所选内容或使用情况，不包含账户系统、广告、跟踪服务、远程分析或会收集用户数据的第三方 SDK。

联系方式

English

Effective date: July 27, 2026

ScrollOne respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how selected photos, screen recordings, and related information are handled when you use the app.

Content You Choose

The app processes only the photos or screen recordings you select through the iOS system photo picker. It does not browse or read your entire photo library.

If you choose to save a processed image to Photos, the app requests permission to add photos only. This permission is used solely to save a result you explicitly choose to keep.

On-Device Processing

Long-image creation, content review, and hidden-area processing take place on your device. ScrollOne does not upload your selected photos or screen recordings to developer-operated servers, and it does not use remote analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking services.

Original Content and Temporary Files

ScrollOne does not modify your original photos or screen recordings. The app may create temporary files while processing a recording or preparing content for sharing. These files are cleared when the current task ends or the system share sheet closes.

A processed image is added to your photo library only when you explicitly choose to save it. You remain in control of the saved image.

System Share Sheet and Third-Party Apps

A processed image is made available to another app only after you open the iOS system share sheet and choose a destination. The destination app handles that content under its own privacy policy and terms. ScrollOne does not control that third-party processing.

Limits of Automatic Review

Review before you share. Automatic review may miss content that should be hidden, and it may suggest areas that do not need hiding. Suggested areas are not confirmed privacy issues.

Before sharing or saving, review every suggestion, hidden area, and the full image. You can add or adjust hidden areas manually.

Data Collection and Tracking

The current version does not collect your personal data, selected content, or usage information. It contains no account system, advertising, tracking services, remote analytics, or third-party SDKs that collect user data.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or ScrollOne, contact:

gdni_support@163.com
